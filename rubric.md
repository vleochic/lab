## **Digital Lab Notebook Grading Rubric (100 points)**

The digital lab notebook documents laboratory work corresponding to the chapters of the BIOL 110 lab manual. Each student submits **their own digital notebook** via a web link.

### **Grading Criteria**

**1. Completeness and Organization (30 points)**

- At least 5 lab chapters from our lab manual are included.
- Entries are clearly organized by date and/or lab topic.
- Notebook structure is easy to navigate and logically arranged.

**2. Accuracy and Scientific Content (30 points)**

- Correct recording of procedures, observations, and results.
- Appropriate use of scientific terminology.
- Content aligns with lab manual instructions and course material.

**3. Data Presentation and Documentation (20 points)**

- Data tables, figures, and/or images are clearly labeled.
- Observations and results are presented clearly and legibly.
- Conclusions or summaries reflect the recorded data.

**4. Clarity and Professionalism (10 points)**

- Writing is understandable and coherent.
- Notebook demonstrates care, consistency, and professionalism.

**5. Timely Submission and Functional Link (10 points)**

- Correct web address submitted by the stated deadline.
- Digital notebook is accessible and functional.

## **Research Presentation Grading Rubric (75 points)**

The presentation is an **individual delivery** based on biology research using peer‑reviewed or academic sources.

### **Grading Criteria**

**1. Scientific Content and Research Quality (25 points)**

- Topic is appropriate to biology.
- Information is accurate and supported by reputable academic sources.
- Concepts are explained clearly and correctly.

**2. Organization and Structure (15 points)**

- Clear introduction, body, and conclusion.
- Logical flow of ideas.
- Time is used effectively.

**3. Visual Aids (Slides or Media) (15 points)**

- Visuals enhance understanding (figures, diagrams, charts).
- Slides are readable, uncluttered, and well‑designed.
- Sources are cited on slides where appropriate.

**4. Oral Delivery and Communication (10 points)**

- Speaker is audible, clear, and understandable.
- Appropriate pacing and engagement with the audience.
- Demonstrates preparation and confidence.

**5. Submission and Accessibility (10 points)**

- Functional web link submitted by the deadline.
- Presentation is accessible and viewable without technical issues.

## **Research Article Grading Rubric (125 points)**

Each student submits an **individual biology research article** supported by peer‑reviewed or academic sources.

### **Grading Criteria**

**1. Scientific Accuracy and Depth (40 points)**

- Content is scientifically correct and relevant.
- Demonstrates understanding of biological concepts.
- Provides sufficient depth and explanation appropriate for college level.

**2. Use and Integration of Sources (25 points)**

- Uses appropriate peer‑reviewed journals and/or academic references.
- Sources support claims and are integrated smoothly into the text.
- Citations are consistent and complete.

**3. Organization and Structure (25 points)**

- Clear structure (introduction, main sections, conclusion).
- Logical progression of ideas.
- Paragraphs are focused and coherent.

**4. Writing Quality and Clarity (20 points)**

- Writing is clear, precise, and readable.
- Proper grammar, spelling, and sentence structure.
- Scientific terminology is used correctly.

**5. Formatting and Submission (15 points)**

- Article is formatted consistently and professionally.
- Functional web link submitted by deadline.
- Overall presentation reflects care and effort.

## **Tutorial Assignment Grading Rubric (100 points)**

Each student submits **at least one tutorial** explaining how to complete a task relevant to BIOL 110.

### **Grading Criteria**

**1. Relevance and Purpose (25 points)**

- Tutorial addresses a clearly defined BIOL 110‑related task.
- Content is appropriate and useful for other students.

**2. Clarity and Instructional Effectiveness (30 points)**

- Steps are clearly explained and easy to follow.
- Instructions are logically sequenced.
- Examples, visuals, or demonstrations are used effectively (if applicable).

**3. Accuracy and Completeness (20 points)**

- Instructions are correct and complete.
- Tutorial fully explains how to accomplish the task.

**4. Organization and Presentation (15 points)**

- Tutorial is well organized and easy to navigate.
- Writing or media is polished and professional.

**5. Submission and Accessibility (10 points)**

- Functional web link submitted by the deadline.
- Tutorial is accessible and viewable without technical issues.
